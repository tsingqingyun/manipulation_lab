LabClassroomSans-Regular.otf、LabClassroomSans-Bold.otf、LabClassroomSerif-Regular.otf。

These are the Simplified Chinese (SC) faces of Noto Sans CJK and Noto Serif CJK,
extracted from system TTC files and renamed to Lab Classroom Sans / Serif.
Shared CFF subfont names retain JP labels even in the SC face; those internal
labels are also renamed for clear PDF identification. All character maps and
glyph programs are unchanged, verified by prepare_fonts.py and recorded in
